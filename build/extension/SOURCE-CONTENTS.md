# Source package contents (Lletrium! 1.0.1)

This package includes the loadable extension under `build/extension/`, the shared engine source and generators under `engine-src/`, selected upstream references under `references/`, and license/provenance notices. The development-only `analysis/` workspace and its comparison oracles are not included.

The Catalan, Spanish and American English shared-engine pipelines are rebuildable from this ZIP with `node engine-src/build.mjs` (Node.js 20+). Their required generated inventories and extracted spelling inputs are included under `engine-src/build-inputs/`; the necessary upstream source trees are under `references/`. The development-only `analysis/` tree and its full comparison oracles are not required by the main build and are omitted. French stays a separate proof-stage runtime integration: its adapter sources are under `engine-src/modules/french/`, its runtime resources and generated rule-candidate table are under `engine-src/resources/fr/`, and `engine-src/build.mjs` stages those files into `build/extension/`. The selected LanguageTool French module source is preserved under `references/languagetool-fr/`. This source layout makes the French adapter/runtime files reproducible without claiming that the French resources are legally cleared.

French resource rights and exact provenance are not fully resolved. `build/extension/licenses/french-data/RESOURCE-MAP.md` records each known pending item; these remain explicitly pending. Including the upstream snapshot for review does not certify redistribution rights or change any pending status. Keep its upstream notices and the applicable third-party terms intact.

The language `.pack` archives are external to this source ZIP and are maintained separately in the `language-packs` branch.
